# Paper archive

This directory stores local reading copies of the four source-benchmark papers,
the most relevant privacy-related work, and same-type tool-use benchmark
candidates. Canonical pages and relevance notes are recorded in
`../benchmark_originals_and_related_work.md` and
`../same_type_benchmark_candidates.md`.

PDF files are downloaded from official proceedings or arXiv endpoints. They are
reading copies; cite the canonical landing pages rather than local filenames.
